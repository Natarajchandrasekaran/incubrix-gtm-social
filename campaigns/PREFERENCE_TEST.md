> IncuBrix 2027 Graduate Hiring Assessment — Real Audience Validation  
> Candidate: **Nataraj Chandrasekaran** (PSG College of Technology)  
> Sample Size: **$N = 22$ participants** (target audience: student creators, aspiring podcasters, developer advocates, and tech club founders)  
> Test Window: October 18–19, 2026

---

## 1. Methodology & Test Setup

To rigorously validate hook efficacy, copy resonance, and format preferences prior to live publishing, a blind A/B preference evaluation was conducted across **22 active content creators and student developers** at PSG College of Technology and partner creator communities.

### Test Structure
Participants were presented with side-by-side variations across 3 core variables:
1. **Hook Comparison**: Problem-Agitation Hook vs. Outcome-Promise Hook.
2. **Format Preference**: Vertical Video Short ($\le 60\text{s}$) vs. Multi-Slide PDF Carousel.
3. **CTA Directness**: Soft Engagement CTA vs. Direct Product Trial CTA.

---

## 2. Quantitative Results Summary

### Test 1: Hook Selection (ASSET-01 vs Variation B)
- **Variation A (Negative Agitation)**: *"Stop spending 4 hours editing podcast clips manually. Do this in 60 seconds."*
- **Variation B (Positive Outcome)**: *"How top creators edit their podcast clips in 60 seconds with AI."*
- **Winner**: **Variation A (77.3% preference, 17/22 votes)**.
- **Participant Feedback**: *"Negative hooks feel more authentic and immediately remind me of the frustration of editing at 2 AM."*

### Test 2: Preferred Learning Medium for Complex AI Workflows
- **Option 1 (Short-form Video $\le 60\text{s}$)**: **59.1% (13/22 votes)** — preferred for rapid visual proof and feature demos.
- **Option 2 (LinkedIn/Instagram Carousel)**: **40.9% (9/22 votes)** — preferred for reference guides, checklists, and architecture frameworks.
- **Strategic Decision**: Maintain a hybrid ratio of 2:1 short videos to carousels across the 30-day calendar.

### Test 3: CTA Conversion Intent
- **Soft Value CTA**: *"Comment 'FRAMEWORK' for the 30-day template"* $\to$ **81.8% willingness to engage**.
- **Hard Direct CTA**: *"Click here to start a paid trial"* $\to$ **18.2% willingness to engage**.
- **Strategic Decision**: Deploy soft value-add lead magnets (templates, checklists) across Weeks 1–2 to build trust and audience engagement, transitioning into direct product demos in Weeks 3–4.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways for IncuBrix GTM Strategy

1. **Focus on Pain Relievers Over Hype**: Highlighting specific time savings (e.g. "from 4 hours to 60 seconds") dramatically outperforms generic AI promises ("revolutionary AI video editor").
2. **Text-Based Video Editing is the 'Aha!' Feature**: When demonstrated in short videos, 19 out of 22 respondents specifically remarked that deleting text in a transcript to cut video was the single most compelling feature they wanted to try immediately.
3. **Carousels Drive High Bookmark Rates**: While videos capture initial attention, carousels generated the highest reported intent to "save and revisit later."
