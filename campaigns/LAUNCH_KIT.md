## 5. Risk Assessment & Mitigation

| Potential Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Preventive & Reactive Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Platform Algorithm Reach Suppression** | Medium | Medium | Stagger formats across carousels, text, and shorts; engage in niche comments within the first 60 minutes of posting to stimulate distribution. |
| **Audience Drop-Off on External Links** | High | Low | Always place direct link in post comments (LinkedIn) or bio/description (Instagram/YouTube), never interrupting native consumption in the first line. |
| **Negative AI Sentiment** | Low | Medium | Reiterate our human-in-the-loop editorial principle: "AI eliminates repetitive editing grunt work so creators have more time for original thinking." |
