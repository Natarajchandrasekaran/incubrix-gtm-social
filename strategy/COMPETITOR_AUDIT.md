## 3. Five Critical Gaps in Competitors' Social Strategies

1. **High Pricing Paywalls Agitate Creators**:
   - Descript and Opus Clip push users onto $20–$35/month tiers almost immediately after 1 free export.
   - *IncuBrix Advantage*: Highlight generous free-tier creator features (like text-based cutting and stem editing) with ₹0 upfront cost.

2. **Generic Stock B-Roll Burnout**:
   - Submagic and Munch frequently insert irrelevant, repetitive stock clips that viewers quickly identify as AI spam.
   - *IncuBrix Advantage*: Emphasize context-aware B-roll insertion and creator editorial control (Human-in-the-Loop).

3. **Weak Founder / Engineering Storytelling**:
   - Competitors rely exclusively on slick corporate marketing. None of them show the raw engineering, latency optimizations, or build-in-public journey.
   - *IncuBrix Advantage*: Dual Singapore HQ + Tiruchirappalli engineering talent narrative resonates heavily with developers, tech founders, and agency leads.

4. **Lack of Carousel Reference Assets on LinkedIn**:
   - Most competitors focus solely on vertical video, ignoring the fact that B2B founders and marketing heads bookmark PDF slide decks and architecture frameworks.
   - *IncuBrix Advantage*: 5 dedicated visual carousels in our 30-day suite specifically targeting LinkedIn decision-makers.

5. **Disjointed Workflow**:
   - Creators must use Descript for editing, Submagic for captions, and Buffer for scheduling.
   - *IncuBrix Advantage*: Single unified platform offering text editing, voice cloning, stems/music, and 1-click multi-publishing to YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, and Bluesky.
