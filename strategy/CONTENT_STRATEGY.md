## 2. Multi-Platform Channel Distribution

IncuBrix will maintain active distribution across **4 core platforms** (exceeding the 3-platform requirement):

1. **LinkedIn**:
   - Focus: Deep-dive PDF Carousels, text frameworks, founder build-in-public stories.
   - Pacing: 5 posts per week (Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM IST / 11:00 AM SGT).
   - Metric: Profile impressions, comment depth, outbound demo clicks.
2. **Instagram**:
   - Focus: Fast Reels ($\le 60\text{s}$), visual infographics/carousels, UI feature teasers.
   - Pacing: 4 posts per week (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday, Sunday).
   - Metric: Saves, shares, bio link clicks.
3. **YouTube Shorts**:
   - Focus: Tactical $\le 60\text{s}$ tutorials, high-retention screen demonstrations, algorithm breakdowns.
   - Pacing: 5 shorts per week.
   - Metric: Average Percentage Viewed (APV $\ge 75\%$), subscriber conversions.
4. **X / Twitter**:
   - Focus: Engineering architecture threads, contrarian creator takes, data breakdowns.
   - Pacing: 3 threads + 4 standalone posts weekly.
   - Metric: Retweets, bookmarks, website clicks.

### Pillar 1: Product Demo / How-To (35% of total volume)
- Demonstrates immediate product utility.
- Eliminates software friction by showing step-by-step feature execution.
- Primary CTA: Free trial registration at `app.incubrix.com`.

### Pillar 2: Creator Growth & Repurposing Strategy (30% of total volume)
- Educates creators on retention mechanics, hook writing, and distribution frameworks.
- Positions IncuBrix as thought leaders rather than just another software vendor.
- Primary CTA: Comment trigger for free PDF templates and checklists.

### Pillar 3: Industry Insights & AI Tooling (20% of total volume)
- Deconstructs modern creator technology (voice cloning, speech-to-speech models, transcription latency).
- Debunks myths (e.g. AI replacing humans vs AI eliminating repetitive grunt work).
- Primary CTA: Engagement, discussion, and social sharing.

### Pillar 4: Founder & Behind the Scenes (15% of total volume)
- Builds authentic human connection and brand affinity.
- Highlights IncuBrix dual footprint (Singapore HQ + Tiruchirappalli engineering).
- Primary CTA: Career applications, community following, direct feedback.

## 4. Brand Voice & Editorial Guidelines

- **Pragmatic & Technical**: Explain how things work under the hood without overwhelming jargon.
- **No-Fluff & Action-Oriented**: Cut intro small talk; dive straight into the hook within the first 3 seconds or first 2 lines.
- **Empowering, Not Alarmist**: Frame AI as an accelerator for authentic human insight, not a tool for mass-producing spam.
- **Transparent Attribution**: Adhere strictly to commercial rights, no copyrighted music, and honest disclosure of AI workflows.
